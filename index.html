How to Play

  • WASD / arrows — fly your wisp. Touch: drag anywhere to steer.
  • Enemy bullets come in cyan and magenta. Your wisp has a polarity — shown by its colour and the HUD chip.
  • Space / Shift — swap polarity instantly. Touch: the SWAP button.
  • Bullets that match your polarity are absorbed — they build charge and score. Bullets of the opposite polarity damage your hull.
  • X / click — nova (needs a full charge bar). Clears every bullet and damages every enemy. Touch: the NOVA button.
  • There is no gun — the nova is your only weapon. Absorb to earn it, spend it when the sky is full.
  • P pause · M mute · R restart sector.
  • Clear a sector to unlock the next. Your best score per sector is saved.
  • Watch the snipers: they glow and draw an aim line before firing a fast shot. Spinner rings mix both colours — no single polarity is safe inside one.
